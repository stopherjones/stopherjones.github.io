About Me

Hi, I'm Chris (stopherjones), a hobby developer with a few simple, open-source, static projects hosted on GitHub and GitHub Pages.

All of my web projects were built for personal use, but made available as open-source projects should they be of wider use / interest - view all my project repos on my GitHub profile

  • Daily games - A simple list of daily games and puzzles I like to play.
  • Record Collection - My virtual record collection.
  • Around the world - Exploring the world through books and music.
  • Media search - Aggregated audiobook and world music search.
  • Sports digest - Aggregated sports tracker and results digest.
  • Portfolio - My photos and drawings.
  • Birds - Photographing the birds of the UK.
  • Around the world in eighty games - A project to play 80 games around the world on Board Game Arena.
  • Play around the world - Explore the world on BGA.
  • BGA tournament tracker - Keep on top of TBA tournaments on BGA.
  • Beatbox - Make beats with a simple pattern sequencer and looper.

I do not collect any personal information, or use any cookies, ads or tracking tools.
